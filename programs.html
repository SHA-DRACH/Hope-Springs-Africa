The projects

What we fund, and why

01 · Health

Water Filters

For a family, a clean water filter means healthier children, fewer illnesses, and more time for education and work.

Fund a filter
02 · Education

Education Essentials

School uniforms, backpacks, tuition, umbrellas and rain gear — removing barriers to learning and keeping children safe during wet seasons.

Send a child to school
03 · Dignity

Menstrual Health

Access to feminine hygiene products, soap, toothpaste and lotions is crucial for girls’ well-being and education.

Support a girl
04 · Opportunity

College Scholarships

Tuition assistance in partnership with the University of Kagili, supporting long-term growth and opportunity for selected students.

Back a scholar
05 · Shelter

Home Renovations

Roof repairs, toilet rebuilding and improved living conditions for vulnerable families — restoring dignity and safety.

Repair a home
06 · Relief

Emergency Help Fund

Swift relief — food, health insurance, mattresses, basins, toiletries and jerry cans — for the elderly, widows and families in crisis.
