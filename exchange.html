June 4–11, 2026 · Kigali & Muhunga

Global Creators Exchange

A one-week youth cultural exchange in Rwanda. In just eight days, young leaders gain a lifetime of perspective.

Fill out our Interest Form Support the Exchange

The premise

This is not tourism.
It’s relationship.

Children who learn to see beyond their own experience grow into adults who lead differently. The Global Creators Exchange builds empathy, leadership, and global understanding through meaningful cultural connections.

Eight days

The experience

4 nights

City Immersion

Four nights in Kigali exploring culture, history, and daily life.

3 nights

Village Immersion

Three nights in Muhunga learning alongside local families.

Daily

Creative Exchange

Art, music, games, language, and shared traditions.

Closing

Safari & Reflection

Wildlife conservation and age-appropriate historical learning.

How connection happens

Shared play. Shared story. Shared table.

  • Shared games and cooperative play
  • Art and storytelling
  • Language learning
  • Cultural traditions
  • Faith expressed through love and respect

And after they come home

Leadership continues.

  • School and church presentations
  • Creative storytelling and art
  • Community awareness events
  • Youth-led service initiatives
  • Fundraisers supporting global partnership
