About us

Hope, delivered with consistency.

A faith-based nonprofit bringing hope and transformative change to the beautiful nation of Rwanda — addressing some of the most pressing challenges families face daily.

Mission

Hope Springs Africa is a faith-based nonprofit organization whose mission is to bring hope and transformative change to the beautiful nation of Rwanda. We are dedicated to improving the lives of communities by addressing some of the most pressing challenges they face daily.

Vision

We envision families throughout Africa living a life of dignity and hope for a brighter tomorrow — knowing they have the resources to determine their destiny.

A commitment to transparency, accountability, and sustainable impact drives our work. We partner with African community organizations, ensuring our initiatives promote long-term self-reliance. Together, we can build a world where every person has the opportunity to thrive and where communities are empowered to create their paths toward a better tomorrow.

The model

S.P.R.I.N.G.S.™

Seven commitments that shape every decision we make. Where hope springs, transformation flows.

Sustainable

“We don’t stop at giving—we create systems that grow.”

  • Reusable sanitary pad trainings
  • Water filter maintenance
  • Entrepreneurial workshops

Progress

“Every small step matters.”

  • Children returning to school
  • Parents receiving health insurance
  • Farmers adopting new techniques

Relationship

“We lead with love and walk with people—not ahead of them.”

  • Visiting homes
  • Sitting with families
  • Knowing individuals personally

Innovation

“We embrace creativity rooted in context.”

  • Pig co-ops
  • Beekeeping
  • Sewing initiatives that generate income

Nurture

“We care deeply and serve fully—body, mind, and spirit.”

  • Food, mattresses and soap for urgent needs
  • Ongoing family support

Grace

“We lead with grace and give it freely.”

  • Presence and patience over pressure
  • Recognizing that transformation isn’t rushed

Stewardship

“We don’t just raise funds—we steward futures.”

  • Every initiative is tracked
  • Every initiative is prayed over
  • Every initiative is designed for long-term impact
