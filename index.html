Our mission

A Mission Rooted in Care and Consistency

Hope Springs Africa is a nonprofit serving children and families in rural Rwanda through education and practical programs that help women support their families. We focus on what matters most—education, nutrition, life skills, and community—while building a foundation for long-term growth.

How it began

Finding Sifa

A chance meeting on a rural road. Three weeks of searching to find them again. Two days before flying home, Rhonda reconnected with Yvonne and her children — and made a promise that help and hope were on the way.

Within one month, the children had uniforms, backpacks and paid tuition. The family had books, mattresses and health insurance. Sifa — whose name means the chosen one — became the face of everything that followed.
